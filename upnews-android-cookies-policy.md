# UpNews Cookies Policy — Android

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Applies from app version 1.1.0 (build 2)

UpNews for Android is provided by **South Desert Studio, S.L.** (see the
[UpNews Legal Notice](upnews-android-legal-notice.md)), contactable at `hola@southdesertstudio.com`.
This policy explains the app's position on cookies and similar technologies, as required by article
22.2 of the Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE).

## The short version

**UpNews sets no cookies and uses no similar tracking technology. For that reason the app does not
show a cookie banner.**

UpNews is a native Android app. It has no embedded web browser, keeps no HTTP cookie store, and uses
no advertising identifier, device fingerprinting, tracking pixel, web beacon or analytics SDK.

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps this state in app-private storage, which is strictly necessary for the service you
requested and is not used to track you:

- your settings (language, topics, optional zodiac sign, dimmed background, first-launch status);
- your last approximate position, for up to seven days;
- bounded aggregates for Smart Elevator;
- a cache of news, weather and topic content and of downloaded images, pruned during normal use.

None of it is sent for advertising or tracking, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app.
Automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled.

## Network requests

Requests to the UpNews service, to `ipwho.is` (location fallback) and to image servers are sent
without cookies, session identifiers or advertising identifiers, and are not used to build a
profile. What each request contains is described in the
[UpNews Privacy Policy](upnews-android-privacy-policy.md).

## Links that leave the app

When you open an article, a source or a legal document, UpNews hands the link to your web browser.
From that moment you are on a third-party website, and **that website's own cookies apply under its
own cookie policy**. UpNews cannot set, read or control them; manage them in your browser settings.

## Changes

If a future version of UpNews introduces any technology covered by this policy, this document will
be updated, and consent will be requested where required, before that version is released.
