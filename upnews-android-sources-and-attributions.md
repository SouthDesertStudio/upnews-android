# UpNews Sources and Attributions — Android

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Applies from app version 1.1.0 (build 2)

UpNews, provided by South Desert Studio, S.L., brings together content from the third-party
providers below. We do not write or edit that content. Each provider keeps all rights to its content,
names and trademarks, and its own terms apply. The same list appears in the app under
**Settings › Sources**, with a link to each provider.

Each card shows the name of the provider it comes from. This page gives the full attribution and
licence notice for each provider.
